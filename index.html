About

About Me

I am a statistician and academic with experience in teaching, research, data analysis, and applied statistical modelling. My research interests include Bayesian methods, machine learning, time-series analysis, environmental statistics, extreme-value modelling, and drought-risk assessment.

My work focuses on applying rigorous statistical methods to real-world problems, particularly in climate, environmental, and health-related data.

Research

Research Interests

Selected areas that define my current and developing research agenda.

Bayesian Statistics

Bayesian inference, prior–posterior modelling, uncertainty quantification, and MCMC-based estimation.

Machine Learning

Predictive modelling using regression, decision trees, random forests, support vector machines, and related methods.

Time-Series Analysis

Forecasting, ARIMA-family models, volatility modelling, multivariate time series, and temporal dependence.

Environmental Statistics

Statistical analysis of precipitation, temperature, drought indicators, and other environmental processes.

Extreme-Value Analysis

Modelling rare events, return levels, tail behaviour, and climate extremes using classical and Bayesian methods.

Spatial & Climate Risk

Spatial interpolation, drought-risk mapping, regional climate assessment, and probabilistic risk modelling.
